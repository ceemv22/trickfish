# Piece drawings

The twelve SVG files in this folder are the **Merida** chess pieces by
Armando Hernandez Marroquin, as distributed with Lichess
(https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/merida),
licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later
(https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-2.0.txt).

Modification: the decorative white-to-transparent gradient on the white
fills was removed and replaced by a flat fill, so the pieces print as
flat ink-and-paper diagram figures. The studio recolours them at render
time (ink #15130F, paper #EEEAE0).
