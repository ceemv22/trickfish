## Channel decisions based on first-party rules

Checked 2026-11-08:

- [Lichess forum etiquette](https://lichess.org/page/forum-etiquette) excludes purely promotional posts and asks advertisements to remain outside Lichess. Do not post the offer in its forums or send unsolicited DMs.
- [r/chess rule 6](https://www.reddit.com/r/chess/wiki/rules/) limits self-promotion based on participation history. Its software exception requires free-to-use software, among other conditions. Do not assume a paid Windows offer qualifies merely because its source is GPL. The user's account history has not been checked and no moderator approval exists; no r/chess post is prepared for submission.
- A personal profile or owned project page is the initial path. An active social account and explicit destination-specific posting authorization are still needed before the agent sends a public social post. Do not grant a third-party social integration broader access just to avoid that step.

The goal is one relevant demonstration, not a batch of repetitive posts. We are not resuming cold emails or sales calls.
