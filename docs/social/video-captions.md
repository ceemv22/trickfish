# Short video captions (@trickfishchess)

Fourteen vertical videos, 1080x1920, silent, 16 to 21 seconds. Render them with:

```
python -m trickfish.studio packs/e4e5-white-1200/traps.json --notes packs/e4e5-white-1200/notes.json --output dist --only video --video-traps 1,19,17,12,22,16,3,7,13,20,8,23,6,14
```

Numbers are the ones on the cards (share of games that went wrong, White's score after the most common mistake). Hashtags are at the end, three at most. The pack link is in the profile, not in the caption.

## Posting guide

- **Same file on all three.** Upload the identical mp4 to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Only the caption text changes. Upload natively on each platform, no watermarked re-uploads.
- **Order.** TikTok first (it is the most forgiving for a new account and the fastest to show whether a hook works), YouTube Shorts and Reels the same day or the next. One video per day at most for the first week, so we can see which hook (65% stat or points) holds viewers.
- **Times.** Reuse the playbook slots: 13:00 UTC (Europe after lunch, US East starting its day) and 19:00 UTC (Europe evening, US lunchtime). Start with 13:00 UTC and test 19:00 UTC on the second video. These are reasoned defaults, not measured. Compare views at three seconds after two weeks, then move a slot.
- **Replies.** Same rules as the X playbook: answer questions with the data, say where our numbers stop, never mock a player. Pin a comment with the answer to "what would you play?" only if people guess wrong.
- **Link.** Set the profile link on all three platforms to the Gumroad free sampler as soon as it exists. Until then the link in bio points at the pack page, and the captions' "link in bio" still holds. Check the link works from a phone after every change.
- **Check before posting.** Watch the first and last frames on a phone: the countdown must read, and nothing important should sit under the platform buttons (we keep 150 px at the top and 400 px at the bottom clear).

## Sound

- Our own sound effects are baked into every mp4: wooden piece taps (heavier on captures, a faint second tick on checks), a soft countdown blip, a reel ratchet while the red line grows, a small tink on the hook and a warm chord on the end card. All of it is synthesised from scratch by `src/trickfish/studio/sound.py`, so nothing is sampled or copyrighted. No ambient bed: silence between events. One static gain, about -16 to -19 LUFS integrated, true peak under -1 dBTP. `--silent` makes the old silent version.
- In each app the owner may add a trending or library sound at about 10-20% volume under ours: TikTok and Instagram Reels, the app's own licensed music; YouTube Shorts, the Shorts audio library. Never add copyrighted music outside the apps.
