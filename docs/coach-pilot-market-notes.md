# Pilot pricing notes

Checked 2026-11-05 against public first-party pages. This is product research, not evidence of demand for Trickfish.

| Adjacent service | Published scope / price | Implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Aimchess](https://aimchess.com/) | Personalized game analytics and training; Premium USD 7.99 monthly, USD 57.99 annually; free tier available. | A low-cost automated alternative exists. These subscription prices do not validate a bespoke repertoire service price. |
| [ChessBase opening report](https://help.chessbase.com/cbase/17/eng/openings_report.htm) | Documentation describes main and critical lines, repertoire and plans. No current purchase price inferred from this documentation. | Opening reports are an established category. Exporting engine lines alone is insufficient differentiation. |

Trickfish's proposed test is a small, prepared repertoire review with transparent candidate rejection and model-response evidence. The coach must establish whether that evidence adds value to existing tools. No competitor superiority claim is supported.

The proposed EUR 29 five-position pilot is an internal experiment, not a currency conversion, market average or validated willingness to pay. Begin with a free existing demonstration, then ask for an explicit scoped order. Record refusals and the actual preparation cost alongside positive responses. Do not infer a revenue timeline from coding velocity.
